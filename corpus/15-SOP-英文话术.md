# SOP · 英文触达话术

> 来源：领英海外拓客 SOP（精简版）· 已脱敏

七、英文话术（8 条）
把 [ ] 里的内容换成真实信息。所有话术都不含链接——链接只在对方明确要资料、且已有一轮来回之后再发，一次不超过 1 个。
话术 1 · 加好友请求备注
Hi I'm [发送人] from [公司], I'm reaching out regarding the [活动全名]. Hope we can connect and share with you our latest agenda.
话术 2 · 通过后的第一条消息（最常用）
Hi , nice to connect! 
We are preparing our [活动全名] [活动日期]. Our confirmed speaker lineup consists of senior leaders from [演讲嘉宾公司列表] etc., including ASEAN Regional Heads, Solution Architects, R&D Directors and CEOs.
Session topics cover AIDC power‑capacity expansion, real‑world liquid‑cooling deployment cases, CDU solutions, 800VDC high‑voltage DC architecture, and thermal design for high‑power AI chips.
Please share your email address if interested in more details, and I will send you the official invitation letter with registration form. Or you can fill in the attached registration form directly, and I will register you.
Best regards,
[发送人]
话术 3 · 跟踪 D+3（换角度）
Hi [First Name], hope you are great! Just would like to circle back — is this summit something you're looking for, or is it parked for now?
话术 4 · 最后跟踪（礼貌收尾）
Hi [First Name], I don't want to keep cluttering your message box — I'll leave it here. If this event becomes a priority later, just message me and I'll pick it up. If you are not interested in this event at all, please let me know and we can stay connected for other events. Wishing you a good week.
话术 5 · 点赞 / 评论过的客户开场
Hi [First Name], thanks for thumb-up on our event poster. I'm [发送人] from [公司], I'm reaching out regarding the [活动全名] [活动日期]. Is this relevant to what you're working on and current focus?
分下一条回复：
Our confirmed speaker lineup consists of senior leaders from [演讲嘉宾公司列表] etc., including ASEAN Regional Heads, Solution Architects, R&D Directors and CEOs.
Session topics cover AIDC power‑capacity expansion, real‑world liquid‑cooling deployment cases, CDU solutions, 800VDC high‑voltage DC architecture, and thermal design for high‑power AI chips.
Should you be interested, please share your email address. I will send you the official invitation letter together with the full agenda for your event registration.
话术 6 · 索取邮箱 + 意向确认
Could you please share your email address with us? Our team will send the full agenda to you via email.
重要的客户发：
Thank you for your interest! Our team will send the registration invitation and full agenda email to your mailbox.
We would also like to learn: what are your key topics of interest for this event, and what types of companies you hope to connect with?
话术 7 · 回传表邮件 / 注册确认 / 退出请求
回传表邮件，主题：[Event Name] — registration form for [Company]
Hi [First Name], Thanks for confirming your interest in [活动全名] [活动日期]. The registration form is attached — it takes about two minutes to fill in and secure the seat first. Could you send it back by [给三天时间]?
注册成功确认，主题：Registration confirmed — [Event Name]
Hi [First Name], You're all set — your registration for [活动全名] [活动日期] at [活动酒店] is confirmed. I'll send a reminder a week before the event.
收到退出请求
Understood — I've removed you from our list and won't contact you again. Apologies for the interruption, and thank you for letting me know.
收到退出请求 → 状态改「已拒绝」，合规标记写 DNC，此后永久不再联系。
